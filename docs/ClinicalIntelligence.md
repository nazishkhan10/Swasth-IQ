# Phase 6 — Clinical Intelligence Engine

The Clinical Intelligence Engine applies rule-based deterministic medical logic to validated parameters.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Patient Summary & Doctor Summary**
   - High-level layperson explanation for patients.
   - Comprehensive clinical review for healthcare professionals.

2. **Deterministic Risk Rules Engine**
   - **Anemia Module**: Evaluates Hemoglobin, RBC, Hematocrit, MCV, MCH, MCHC, and Iron panel.
   - **Diabetes Module**: Evaluates Fasting Blood Glucose, Postprandial Glucose, and HbA1c.
   - **Renal Module (CKD)**: Evaluates Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen, eGFR, and Uric Acid.
   - **Thyroid Module**: Evaluates TSH, Free T3, and Free T4.
   - **Lipid Module**: Evaluates Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL, and VLDL.
   - **Inflammation Module**: Evaluates High Sensitivity CRP (hs-CRP) and ESR.

3. **Organ Health Scores**
   - Quantified percentage scores (0-100%) across key organ systems: Cardiovascular, Renal, Hepatic, Endocrine, Hematological.

4. **Interactive Knowledge Graph & Timeline**
   - Relationships between biomarkers, organ systems, and potential medical conditions.
   - Historical trend analysis across multiple lab reports.
